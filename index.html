    How a purchase works

    1. Visit the property

      See the house or walk the plot with Mr Pelumi. Ask anything about the area, the neighbours and the access road.

    2. Check the documents

      Go through the survey plan and title documents together. Have your lawyer verify them at the land registry before you commit.

    3. Agree terms and pay

      Settle the price in writing, then pay by bank transfer to the named seller or company account and keep every receipt.

    4. Complete and take possession

      Sign the transfer documents, collect your copies and receive the keys or the plot beacons.
